How to play

  • You're given a strand of ~20 letters.
  • Type any valid dictionary word whose letters appear left-to-right in the strand and press Enter.
  • The leftmost occurrence of that word is removed; the strand collapses.
  • Score: 5 points per letter. A 4-letter word = 20, 5-letter = 25, 6-letter = 30.
  • The strand hides bonus words (you see their lengths). The bonus pill turns yellow while findable and red once its letters have been pulled.
  • Game ends when no valid word can be formed from the remaining letters — or after 3 wrong pulls (a non-word or a word with letters not in order).
  • No timer. No reflexes. Pure vocabulary and a sharp eye.
