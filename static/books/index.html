Judith Jameson Must Die

Judith Jameson is drowning in the monotony of an ordinary life, until she stumbles upon a sinister conspiracy buried in her company’s files.

She finds herself targeted by two mysterious men: Brandon, a soldier from the future and Luke, a fanatic from a shadowy political group. Judith is soon caught in a deadly game of cat-and-mouse, forced to flee her home and confront a terrifying truth: she is the key to stopping a future tyrant called Zero, whose crusade of terror will cost three million lives.

Whisked through time to escape a government organization called the Temporal Oversight Agency, Judith and Brandon find themselves battling Mayan warriors, surviving in a prehistoric jungle, and fighting alongside samurai in feudal Japan. As their love deepens against impossible odds, they must confront the ultimate question: what is the price for saving three million lives?
